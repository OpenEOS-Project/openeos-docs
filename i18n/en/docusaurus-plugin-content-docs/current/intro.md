# OpenEOS Manual

**OpenEOS** (Open Event Ordering System) is a modern point-of-sale system for clubs, events, and catering operations. With OpenEOS you manage your events, products, and prices, accept orders at mobile tills, control kitchen and output printers, and monitor revenue in real time.

This manual guides you through the application step by step — from registration through to end-of-event reporting.

## What you can do with OpenEOS

- **Create events** and manage products, prices, and stations per event
- **Choose an operating mode** – [Orders only, Cashless or All features](./betriebsarten.md), depending on how your club handles money
- **Set up tills** on smartphones and tablets — no specialist hardware required
- **Servers** without an account of their own sign in to shared tills with a PIN and are settled at the end of their shift
- **Tables** laid out as a floor plan, opened, served and settled at the till
- **Accept orders**, process payments, and forward them to the kitchen or serving station and to displays
- **Manage deposits** — issue and collect deposit items at the till
- **Keep a cash book** – change, cash in, cash out and cash count wherever your till accepts cash
- **Print receipts** via ESC/POS printers with your own templates
- **Export reports** on revenue, top products, and payment methods
- **Organize your team and shifts** and assign permissions per module
- **Import your menu** with the [assistant](./assistent.md) and get matching product icons suggested
- **Connect other systems** – card payments with SumUp, the [Cloud TSE](./integrationen/cloud-tse.md) for cash in Germany (coming soon), [webhooks](./integrationen/webhooks.md), [API access](./api-zugaenge.md) and [AI agents](./ki-agenten.md)

## How this handbook is arranged

The handbook is a path, not a reference. Work through the chapters once from top to bottom and you end up with a running system:

1. **Account and organization** – register, sign in, create your club, choose the operating mode.
2. **Setting up an event** – set up the event, enter your range, define stations and tables. This is also where **what it costs** is explained, and how to test for free beforehand.
3. **Devices and displays** – connect tills, set up displays, attach printers.
4. **On the day** – sell, keep the cash book, and keep an eye on orders.
5. **After the event** – report, take inventory and settle.

What you need only occasionally — deposits, discount vouchers, members, servers, shift plans, the assistant, settings — sits under **Reference**. Connections to other systems are under **Integrations**, running OpenEOS on your own server under **Self-hosting**.

:::tip[If you are in a hurry]
Chapters 1 to 3 and one test order are enough to get a feel for it. The event stays in test mode and costs nothing.
:::
