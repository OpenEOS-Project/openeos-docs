# Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is your home page after logging in. It gives you a quick overview of your organization's current business activity.

![Dashboard](/img/screens/en/dashboard.png)

## First steps and notices {/* #erste-schritte */}

While your setup is not finished yet, the **First steps** card at the top lists the steps **Create the event**, **Enable selling**, **Create categories**, **Create products** and **Pair a till**. **Go** takes you to the matching page, and completed steps are ticked off. **Hide** closes the card.

Below it, notices can appear:

- **Country and operating mode** – admins see here when the organization's country is missing or the [operating mode](./betriebsarten.md#bestand) still has to be chosen.
- **Emergency support on your event day** – once your organization has booked an event, the phone number for critical problems is shown here as long as the event is **Planned** or **Active**. See [Support](./support.md#emergency-support).

## Period

At the top right you choose what the figures refer to: **Today**, **7 days** or **Event** (the days of the active event). All tiles follow this choice.

## Tiles

The dashboard is made of tiles. By default you see:

| Tile | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Orders** | Number of orders in the chosen period |
| **Revenue** | Revenue in the chosen period |
| **Active events** | Number of currently running events |
| **Active users** | Users currently signed in |
| **System status** | Reachable tills, printers, print queue and open orders |

You add more tiles via **Customise**: **Top products**, **Revenue by hour**, **Payment methods**, **Low stock** and **Activity** (orders, payments and printing in real time).

## Recent Activity

Below the tiles, the **Recent Activity** section lists your latest orders with time, amount and status. As long as no orders have been recorded yet, a notice appears here — start an event to begin accepting orders.

## Customizing the dashboard

Click **Customise** at the top right. The tiles get a handle for moving, a corner at the bottom right for resizing and an **×** for removing. **Add tile** brings in more tiles. **Done** ends editing; the layout is saved for your account.

![Customise dashboard](/img/screens/en/dashboard-customize.png)

## Active event

At the top left of the sidebar, below your organization's name, you can see the currently **active event** with its phase: **Test mode**, **Planned · from …** (with the sales start), **Active** or **Ended**. In test mode and while it is planned it also carries a **TEST** badge. Clicking it opens the [events](./veranstaltungen.md#phasen). Many areas — such as products, stations and inventory — always refer to the currently active event. If none is active, it reads **No active event** — activate an event first (see [Events](./veranstaltungen.md#activating-an-event)).
